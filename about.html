About FastMenuPrice

Welcome to FastMenuPrice, an independent online consumer resource dedicated to publishing clean, transparent fast-food pricing, catering calculations, and printable menu cards.

Our Mission

Finding accurate pricing for large catering orders (like Raising Cane's Tailgate boxes or Panda Express party trays) can be frustrating on official restaurant apps that force users through multi-step checkout processes. We aggregate, verify, and format menu prices into simple tables and printable PDF files to help families, students, and event planners budget efficiently.

Editorial Accuracy

Our editorial team monitors restaurant price updates across US and Canadian franchise locations. Prices are gathered from direct restaurant visits, retail receipts, and user submissions.
